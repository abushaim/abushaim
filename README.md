## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Abu Shaim**, a Bangladesh-based entrepreneur, WordPress designer and developer, and digital marketing consultant. I work across **web technologies, digital marketing, creative production, business operations, and AI-powered automation**.

My focus is simple: build useful solutions, improve workflows, and help businesses operate more efficiently.

- 🌐 **Web development:** WordPress, responsive websites, and front-end experiences
- 📈 **Digital marketing:** SEO, social media, lead generation, and content strategy
- 🤖 **AI & automation:** Workflow automation, document organization, and content production systems
- ⚙️ **Business operations:** CRM workflows, customer communication, and process improvement
- 🎓 **Education:** MBA
- 📍 **Based in:** Bangladesh
- 🔗 **My website:** [abushaim.com](https://abushaim.com/)
